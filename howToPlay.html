How To Play

You take on the role of a government leader trying to balance three competing priorities:
🌎 Climate: Protect the environment and reduce the risk of climate disasters.
💰 Economy: Keep businesses growing and citizens employed.
👥 Public Support: Maintain the trust and approval of the people.
Your main goal will be to keep the government running till 2100. Each round is five years of time
Each round, you will be presented with a policy decision. Every policy will come with trade-offs.
A decision that helps the economy may hurt the climate.
A policy that benefits the environment may be unpopular with voters.
After selecting a policy, you'll immediately see how your decision affects your nation's Climate, Economy, and Public Support scores.
As the game continues, the consequences of your choices will accumulate, making it increasingly difficult to keep all metrics high
